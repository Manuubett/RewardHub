1Introduction

Welcome to SemaRewards, a rewards platform powered by SemaAfrica and operated by DEH EMANUELS SOLUTION.

At SemaRewards, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and share information when you access our website, create an account, participate in activities, earn points or request rewards.

By using SemaRewards, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

Our handling of personal information is intended to comply with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and applicable data protection regulations.

2Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information.

A. Personal information

When you register or use SemaRewards, we may collect:

  • Full name or username.
  • Email address.
  • Mobile phone number.
  • Account identification number.
  • Login and account information.

B. Activity and advertising information

To operate our rewards programme, we may record:

  • Advertising opportunity or button clicked.
  • Unique activity identification.
  • Date and time of activity.
  • User account associated with the activity.
  • Activity completion and review status.
  • Advertising campaign information.
  • Points earned, approved, rejected or adjusted.
  • Cooldown and activity history.

We use this information to monitor platform activity, prevent duplicate submissions, investigate suspicious behaviour and manage eligible rewards.

C. Technical information

We may automatically collect:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type.
  • Operating system.
  • Website usage information.
  • Cookies and similar tracking information.

D. Payment and withdrawal information

When requesting a withdrawal through M-Pesa, we may collect:

  • Registered M-Pesa phone number.
  • Requested withdrawal amount.
  • Points converted into the requested reward.
  • Payment transaction reference.
  • Withdrawal status.

Important: SemaRewards will never ask you to provide your M-Pesa PIN or mobile banking password.

3How we use your information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  1. To create and manage user accounts.
  2. To provide access to SemaRewards activities.
  3. To record and monitor user activities.
  4. To manage points balances and reward transactions.
  5. To review and verify eligible reward requests.
  6. To detect duplicate activities, fraud and suspicious behaviour.
  7. To process approved M-Pesa withdrawals.
  8. To improve website functionality and user experience.
  9. To provide customer support.
  10. To maintain platform security and comply with applicable laws.

4Advertising and activity tracking

SemaRewards may display advertisements, sponsored opportunities and promotional activities provided by third-party advertising partners.

When you interact with an advertising opportunity, our platform may record your activity, including the opportunity identification, account identification and time of interaction. We use activity records to monitor participation and manage the rewards programme.

Please understand that:

  • Clicking a button does not automatically mean an advertisement was successfully displayed.
  • Our internal click records do not independently prove that an advertiser registered a valid event.
  • Advertising providers may separately collect technical and interaction information.
  • Rewards are only available for activities that qualify under the applicable programme and advertising partner rules.
  • We do not guarantee that every activity will result in points or monetary rewards.

Third-party advertising providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Review the relevant provider's policies before interacting with external advertising content.

5Points and rewards

SemaRewards allows eligible users to accumulate points through approved activities. Points are recorded against individual user accounts. Our system may include manual review, activity verification and administrative approval before points are credited.

We may:

  • Review submitted activities.
  • Approve or reject reward claims.
  • Correct inaccurate points transactions.
  • Suspend suspicious activities.
  • Investigate suspected fraudulent behaviour.

Points conversion rates, minimum withdrawal amounts and available rewards are displayed on the platform or communicated through official platform announcements. Points are subject to the SemaRewards Terms and Conditions.

6Sharing of personal information

We do not sell users' personal information. However, we may share necessary information with trusted third parties where required to operate our services. These parties may include:

A. Advertising partners

Advertising partners may receive or independently collect certain information when users interact with their advertisements or sponsored opportunities.

B. Payment service providers

We may share the necessary payment details with payment providers, including M-Pesa service providers, to process approved reward withdrawals.

C. Technology and hosting providers

We may use third-party services to host our website, manage databases, authenticate users, maintain security and provide technical support.

D. Legal and regulatory authorities

We may disclose information where required by law, court order or lawful regulatory request.

We aim to limit information shared with third parties to what is necessary for the relevant purpose.

7Cookies and tracking technologies

SemaRewards may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain user login sessions.
  • Remember user preferences.
  • Improve website performance.
  • Understand website usage.
  • Support security and fraud prevention.
  • Support advertising and analytics where enabled.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features. Where consent is required for optional cookies, we will provide an appropriate consent mechanism.

8Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect users' personal information. These measures may include:

  • Secure account authentication.
  • Access controls.
  • Database security.
  • Administrative permissions.
  • Activity monitoring.
  • Protection against unauthorized access.
  • Backup and recovery procedures.

However, no online platform can guarantee complete security. You are responsible for protecting your password and account access information. Please notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

9Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected. Information relating to accounts, activities, points transactions and withdrawals may be retained for:

  • Account administration.
  • Reward verification.
  • Fraud prevention.
  • Financial record keeping.
  • Legal and regulatory compliance.
  • Resolving disputes.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal obligations.

10International data transfers

Some of our technology, advertising or payment service providers may process information outside Kenya. Where personal information is transferred outside Kenya, we will take the applicable measures required by Kenyan data protection law to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

11Your data protection rights

Under applicable Kenyan data protection law, you may have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal information is used.
  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or misleading information.
  • Request deletion of false or misleading information.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Request restriction of processing where applicable.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details in section 15. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before processing a request.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya: www.odpc.go.ke

12Children's privacy

SemaRewards' points and monetary reward features are intended for adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly permit children to participate in the monetary rewards programme without the appropriate parental or guardian consent and safeguards required by applicable law.

Where SemaAfrica provides educational services to children, their personal information must be handled in accordance with applicable child data protection requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has submitted personal information without the required consent may contact us.

13Third-party websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, advertisements and sponsored opportunities. Once you leave SemaRewards and visit an external website, that website's privacy policy and terms apply.

We do not control the privacy practices, security measures or content of external websites. Review third-party privacy policies before submitting personal information.

14Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:

  • Changes to our services.
  • New advertising partnerships.
  • Changes to payment services.
  • New website features.
  • Changes in applicable laws and regulations.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised update date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

15Contact us

For questions, complaints, privacy concerns or requests concerning your personal information, please contact us.
